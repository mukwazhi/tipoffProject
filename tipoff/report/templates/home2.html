Protecting Aviation Safety Through Ethical Reporting

SkySafe provides a secure, confidential channel for aviation professionals to report safety violations, maintenance concerns, operational misconduct, or regulatory non-compliance. Your courage in speaking up helps maintain the highest standards of safety in our industry.

File a Confidential Report Track A Report

Why Report Through SkySafe?

Strict Confidentiality

Your identity remains protected throughout the process. We use advanced anonymization techniques to ensure your report cannot be traced back to you unless you choose to disclose your identity.

Military-Grade Security

Our platform uses end-to-end encryption meeting aviation industry security standards. All data is stored securely with multiple layers of protection against unauthorized access.

Aviation Expertise

Reports are handled by investigators with aviation operational experience who understand the technical aspects and regulatory framework of our industry.

How to Report Aviation Concerns

1

Prepare Information

Gather relevant details: dates, locations, aircraft/crew involved, and any supporting evidence you can provide without compromising your identity.

2

Choose Reporting Method

Use our secure web form, encrypted email, or dedicated hotline. All methods guarantee confidentiality.

3

Submit Your Report

Provide as much detail as possible. You'll receive a confidential case number for follow-up.

4

Investigation Begins

Our aviation specialists review your concern and initiate appropriate investigation procedures while protecting your identity.

What Should Be Reported

Safety Violations

Deliberate disregard for safety protocols, unauthorized procedure changes, or actions that compromise flight safety.

Maintenance Concerns

Inadequate maintenance, falsified records, deferred critical repairs, or use of unapproved parts.

Regulatory Non-Compliance

Violations of FAA, EASA, or other aviation authority regulations, including duty time limitations.

Ethical Violations

Fraud, bribery, conflicts of interest, or other misconduct that could impact aviation safety or integrity.

Whistleblower Protections

The aviation industry is governed by strong whistleblower protection laws including the Wendell H. Ford Aviation Investment and Reform Act (49 U.S.C. § 42121) and Dodd-Frank Act protections.

Your Rights Include:

  • Confidentiality: We will not disclose your identity without your consent, except when required by law.
  • Non-Retaliation: Federal law prohibits retaliation against whistleblowers in the aviation industry.
  • Legal Remedies: If retaliated against, you may be entitled to reinstatement, back pay, and other damages.
  • Anonymous Reporting: Our system allows completely anonymous reporting while maintaining communication channels.

All reports are handled in accordance with ICAO Annex 19 Safety Management provisions and applicable national laws.
